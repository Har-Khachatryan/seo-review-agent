   - Why: Visitors and crawlers hit dead ends from live pages, which wastes crawl paths and hurts the reader experience on those articles.
   - Next step: Content editors should update the links on the three source pages to working URLs, or the SEO team should add redirects from the dead URLs to the closest live equivalents.
