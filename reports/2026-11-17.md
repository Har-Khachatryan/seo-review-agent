**Status:** AMBER · All 283 sitemap pages load and crawlers can access the site, but 3 internal links return 404 and several pages are missing meta descriptions or H1s.

## Needs attention

1. **Broken internal links point to 404 pages** (high)
   - Why: Visitors and crawlers hit dead ends from live pages, which wastes crawl effort and weakens the internal link paths that search and AI systems follow.
   - Next step: Editors should update the links on the three source pages to working URLs, or the SEO team should set redirects from the dead URLs to their closest live equivalent.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/3-easy-tips-to-creating-content-people-will-share/

   - https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/landing-pages-for-events/
2. **Pages without a meta description** (medium)
   - Why: Without a meta description, search engines and AI answer tools pick their own snippet text, which is often less controlled than a written summary.
   - Next step: Content owners should write unique meta descriptions for these eight pages, starting with the case study and the team bios.

3. **Pages without an H1 heading** (medium)
   - Why: The H1 states the main topic of a page, and its absence makes it harder for search engines and AI tools to understand what the page is about.
   - Next step: Web developers or content owners should add one descriptive H1 to each of these seven templates or pages, checking that the theme does not hide it.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/awards/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/contact-us/

4. **Slow lab load times on two pages with no real-user data** (medium)
   - Why: Lab LCP of 10.9 s and 10.6 s is far above the site-wide real-user LCP of 2.2 s, and these pages have no field data to confirm the experience; lab scores vary between runs.
   - Next step: A developer should check the largest element and any heavy images or scripts on the answer-engine and AI-agency strategy pages, then rerun PageSpeed to confirm the fix.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/answer-engine-optimization/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/strategy/best-ai-seo-agency-companies-services-usa/
5. **Meta descriptions and titles likely truncated in results** (low)
   - Why: Meta descriptions over 165 characters and titles over 65 characters are often cut off in search results, which can reduce how clearly the snippet reads.
   - Next step: Blog editors can shorten the 44 descriptions and 41 titles over time, starting with the pages in the list, as part of normal content updates.
