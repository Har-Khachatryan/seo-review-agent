**Status:** AMBER · All 284 sitemap pages load and crawlers are allowed, but three internal links lead to 404s and today's median server response is about three times its recent baseline.

## Needs attention

1. **Broken internal links point to 404 pages** (high)
   - Why: Visitors and crawlers hit dead ends from live pages, which wastes crawl effort and weakens the internal link paths that help search engines find content.
   - Next step: Content or SEO team: update the links on the three source pages to working URLs, or add redirects from the dead URLs to their closest replacements.

2. **Server response is slower than usual today** (medium, new)
   - Why: Median HTML response rose to 977.5 ms from a 324 ms median over the previous 7 days, which can slow crawling and delay the first content users see.
   - Next step: Developer or hosting team: check server logs and recent deploys or traffic spikes for today, and confirm whether response times return to baseline tomorrow.
3. **Lab LCP is above 10 seconds on three pages** (medium, new)
   - Why: Lab LCP on these pages ran 10.5 to 11.2 s in the worst runs, though these pages have no real-user data and the site-wide real-user LCP is good.
   - Next step: Developer: profile the largest element and render-blocking resources on these pages, then re-run PageSpeed Insights to confirm the fix in the lab.

   - https://www.searchbloom.com/strategy/best-ai-seo-agency-companies-services-usa/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/ecommerce-seo/
4. **Pages missing meta descriptions or H1 headings** (medium)
   - Why: Without a meta description search engines write their own snippet, and without an H1 the main topic of a page is less clear to search engines and AI systems.
   - Next step: Content or SEO team: write unique meta descriptions and add an H1 to each listed page, starting with the public pages such as /awards/ and /contact-us/.
