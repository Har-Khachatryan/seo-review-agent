# SEO review: searchbloom.com, 2026-11-16

**Status:** AMBER · The site is up and healthy overall, but three live blog posts link to dead pages and a few pages are slow or missing on-page basics.

## Needs attention

1. **Three internal links point to 404 pages** (high)
   - Why: Visitors and crawlers hit dead ends from three live posts, which wastes internal link equity and crawl effort.
   - Next step: Editors should update the links on the three source pages to working URLs, or developers should add redirects from the dead URLs.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/brand-building-through-storytelling/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/seo-is-dead/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/blog/5-cro-tips-you-can-use-now/
2. **Answer-engine-optimization page is slow in lab tests** (medium, new)
   - Why: Median lab LCP was 4276 ms across three runs (2631 to 5965 ms), and this page has no real-user data yet.
   - Next step: Developers should check the largest element and image loading on this page, then re-test in PageSpeed Insights.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/answer-engine-optimization/
3. **Best AI SEO agency page has very slow lab LCP** (medium)
   - Why: Lab LCP was 10930 ms with a score of 55, the slowest page in today's test, and two of three runs were above 10 seconds.
   - Next step: Developers should compress or lazy-load the largest above-the-fold assets on this page, then re-run PageSpeed Insights.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/strategy/best-ai-seo-agency-companies-services-usa/
4. **Seven pages have no H1 heading** (medium)
   - Why: Pages without an H1 send weaker topic signals to search engines and AI systems, including hub pages like /strategy/ and /team/.
   - Next step: The content team should add one descriptive H1 to each listed page, and the web team should check the page templates that produce them.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/awards/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/contact-us/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/sitemap/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/team/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/tools/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/compare/
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/strategy/
5. **Eight pages have no meta description** (medium)
   - Why: Without a meta description, search engines write their own snippet, which may not match the page's purpose.
   - Next step: Marketing should add a unique description of about 155 characters or fewer to each listed page in the CMS.
