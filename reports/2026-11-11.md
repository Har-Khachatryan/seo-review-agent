## Needs attention

1. **Broken internal links lead to 404 pages** (high)
   - Why: Visitors and crawlers hit dead ends from live pages, which wastes crawl effort and hurts the user path.
   - Next step: Content editors should update the three links on the linking pages to working URLs, or the SEO or dev team should redirect the dead URLs to the closest live page.

2. **Real-user INP needs improvement site-wide** (medium)
   - Why: Site-wide INP is 266 ms, above the 200 ms good threshold, and the homepage field INP is 236 ms, so page interactions feel slower than they should.
   - Next step: The dev team should review heavy JavaScript on the homepage and the main templates to reduce main-thread work and rerun PageSpeed Insights.
   - https://www.searchbloom.com/
3. **Pages missing a meta description** (medium)
   - Why: Without a meta description, search engines write their own snippet, which may not match the page's purpose.
   - Next step: Content owners should add a unique description of 120 to 160 characters to each listed page, starting with the team bio pages.
